Base hierarchical model and vocabulary: LegalSeg, Shubham Kumar Nigam, Tanmay Dubey, Govind Sharma, Noel Shallum, Kripabandhu Ghosh and Arnab Bhattacharya. Source repository and revision are recorded in model.json. The publisher labels the base model Apache-2.0. This package converts its source checkpoint to safetensors and adds an AttoFlow classifier that writes document-dependent role biases. Research comparison, November 2026.
